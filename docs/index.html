About Us

Zion is a new place of rest in the mountains, where local culture is reinterpreted through architecture, spatial design, hospitality, and contemporary art. Here, old habits take on a new form, and the quiet of the landscape meets modern comfort at every turn of the day, in every season of the year.

Consultation

Concept

The architecture of the complex grows out of the slope, keeping its strength in stone and warmth in wood. Panoramic terraces open toward the valley, and old craft meets new thinking to create another way of resting: slow, meaningful, and close to nature, filled with the energy of the landscape and the culture of the whole region.
